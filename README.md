# POS Reports Sync

POS Reports Sync collects the end-of-shift, day, month and year reports from your Verifone Commander
registers and saves them as files on your PC, automatically, for every store you add.

This page holds the installers. Updates are delivered inside the app; you only download from here
for a new install.

## Requirements
- Windows 10 or 11.
- Verifone Report Navigator installed on the same PC and the same Windows user.
- A POS Reports Sync license key (one per PC).
- Network access from the PC to each store's Commander.

## Install
1. Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download `POSReportsSync-Setup-<version>.exe`
   from **Assets**.
2. Run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click **Install / update**. The app opens when Setup finishes.
4. Click **Enter license key**, paste your key and click **Activate this PC**.
   Until a key is activated the app is view-only.
5. Add your stores and choose where reports are saved.

## Updates
The app checks for updates on its own. When one is ready, a banner shows what's new and an
**Update now** button. Your stores, sign-ins, rules and saved reports are kept.

## Moving to a new PC
In the old PC's app, open **Settings › App** and click **Deactivate this PC**. Then install on the
new PC and enter the same key.

## Support
Contact the person who gave you your license key.
